### A4. Children's information — and your consent

**This is the most important section of this notice.**

Most of the people we hold information about are children. Section 35 of POPIA prohibits processing a child's personal information **unless a competent person — normally a parent or legal guardian — has consented.**

So:

- We will not register a learner, run an assessment, or place a child in a class **until the parent or guardian has given written consent** through our enrolment form.
- Consent is requested **separately for each purpose**. You can agree to the tutoring and decline, for example, the use of your child's photograph in our marketing, and your child will be enrolled exactly the same way.
- **You may withdraw consent at any time**, in writing to our Information Officer. We will stop the relevant processing. Where consent is withdrawn for processing we need in order to teach your child safely, we will tell you what that means for the enrolment before acting on it.
- Where a learner aged 13 or over is given their own login, **the guardian's consent is still what makes the processing lawful.** The learner's own account gives them access to their timetable, their reports and their messages — nothing about siblings, and nothing about billing.

#### Health information

Allergies and medical conditions are **special personal information** under section 26 of POPIA and are subject to a higher standard. We collect them only because we need them to look after your child safely on our premises, and we process them under section 27(1)(a) — your explicit consent — and section 27(1)(d), protection of a vital interest.

Health information is visible **only to the centre manager and administrators**. Tutors are not given access to it through the system. Where a tutor must know something to keep a child safe during a session, the centre manager tells them directly.

### A7. Your information leaves South Africa

Our database is hosted by Supabase in **London, United Kingdom**. This means your and your child's personal information is stored outside South Africa.

Section 72 of POPIA permits this where the receiving country's law, or a binding agreement with the recipient, provides protection substantially similar to POPIA. The United Kingdom's data protection regime meets that standard, and our agreement with Supabase binds them to equivalent safeguards.

By consenting to the enrolment, you consent to this transfer. If you would prefer your child's information not to leave South Africa, contact our Information Officer — we will tell you honestly what we can and cannot offer.

### A8. How long we keep it

We keep learner and account records for **5 years** after a learner leaves the centre. This matches the period we must keep financial records for the South African Revenue Service, and means we can still answer a request for a reference or a historical report.

| Record | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Learner identity, academic records, reports, attendance | 5 years after leaving |
| Health notes and authorised collectors | 5 years after leaving |
| Invoices, payments and accounting records | 5 years from the end of the tax year |
| Tutor employment records | 5 years after employment ends |
| Enquiries that do not become enrolments | 12 months |
| Portal messages | 5 years |
| Marketing consent records | Until withdrawn, then 3 years as proof of the withdrawal |

After the period ends, records are deleted or anonymised so an individual can no longer be identified.

### A9. How we protect it

- Access is controlled at the database level, so a parent's account can only ever return that parent's own learners — this is enforced by the system, not only by the screens.
- Passwords are stored hashed. Nobody at Skolopals can see your password.
- Health notes, identity documents and police clearances are restricted to administrators.
- Uploaded documents are held in private storage that is not publicly reachable.
- All traffic between you and our website is encrypted.
- We log who records a payment, marks a register and publishes a report.

No system is perfectly secure. If a breach affects your information, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as section 22 requires.

### B3. Cross-border transfer assessment (s72)

**Finding.** The Supabase project `dhmjzshlznhqohvkdcpz` is in **eu-west-2 (London)**. All learner data — including children's health information — is stored outside South Africa.

**This is lawful but must be documented.** Section 72 permits the transfer on three independent grounds, and we rely on all three:

1. **s72(1)(a)** — the recipient is subject to a binding agreement providing substantially similar protection. Supabase's DPA, plus UK GDPR, meets this.
2. **s72(1)(b)** — the data subject consents. Captured at enrolment (see B8).
3. **s72(1)(c)** — the transfer is necessary to perform the contract with the data subject.

**Options, if you would rather reduce the exposure:**

| Option | Effect | Cost |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stay in London, rely on s72 (current) | Lawful, documented, nothing to do | None |
| Move the project to a different Supabase region | Supabase has no South African region, so data still leaves SA. No real gain | A migration, no benefit |
| Self-host Postgres in South Africa | Data stays in SA; removes the s72 question entirely | Significant — you lose managed Auth, Storage and backups |

**Recommendation:** stay where you are and rely on s72, with the consent wording in B8. Revisit only if a school partnership or a funder requires local residency.

### B4. Retention implementation

Retention is enforced by the system, not by memory.

- `data_retention_policies(record_class, retention_months, basis)` — seeded from the table in A8
- `learners.left_on` starts the clock; `v_retention_due` lists what is now past its period
- A scheduled purge job runs monthly, writes to `data_retention_log`, and **requires admin confirmation before deleting** — nothing is destroyed silently
- Deletion anonymises rather than drops rows where a record is needed for aggregate KPIs, so historical attendance and revenue figures survive the purge

> **Worth revisiting.** POPIA's minimality principle (s10) argues for holding children's health notes and authorised-collector details for less than 5 years, since neither serves the tax purpose that justifies the 5-year period. You chose a single 5-year rule for simplicity, which is defensible. If you later want the stronger position, shortening just those two record classes is a one-row change to `data_retention_policies`.

### B6. Security compromise procedure (s22)

On discovering or suspecting a breach:

1. **Contain** — revoke keys, suspend affected accounts, stop the leak.
2. **Assess** — within 24 hours, record what was accessed, whose data, and how many people.
3. **Notify the Regulator** — in writing, as soon as reasonably possible.
4. **Notify data subjects** — in writing, unless the Regulator directs otherwise. For learners, notify the guardians. State what happened, what was exposed, what we have done, and what they should do.
5. **Record** in the breach register.
6. **Review** — what changes stop it recurring.

A breach register is maintained from go-live, even if empty.

### B8. Consent capture — what the enrolment form must collect

The enrolment flow (`/enrol`) must record each of these **separately**, into a `consents` table with `consent_type`, `granted`, `granted_at`, `granted_by`, `withdrawn_at` and the policy version:

| Consent | Required to enrol? | Wording |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Processing the learner's personal information (s35) | **Yes** | "I am the parent or legal guardian of this learner and I consent to Skolopals processing their personal information as described in the Privacy Notice." |
| Health and allergy information (s27) | **Yes, if any is given** | "I consent to Skolopals holding my child's health and allergy information so that staff can keep them safe." |
| Storing information outside South Africa (s72) | **Yes** | "I understand that Skolopals' systems are hosted outside South Africa, in the United Kingdom, under protections substantially similar to POPIA." |
| Photographs and schoolwork in marketing | **No — optional** | "I consent to Skolopals using photographs of my child or their work in its marketing. I may withdraw this at any time." |
| WhatsApp and email updates | **No — optional** | "I would like to receive updates about programmes and centre news." |

**The optional consents must default to off and must not block submission.** Bundling them into the enrolment consent is the single most common POPIA failure and would invalidate the consent entirely.

The notice version is stored with each consent, so when the policy changes you know who consented to what.
