Welcome

Skolopals is an after-school tutoring centre in Damonsville, Brits. This manual shows you how to use the Skolopals website and portals, from the day a family applies to the day a learner is signed out at the door.

It follows one family through the whole journey. Thuli Mthembu enrols her daughter Amahle (Grade 6) for Mathematics and Afrikaans. You will see what Thuli does, what Amahle does, what the tutor Johan does and what the centre administrator Lindiwe does. All names, numbers and documents in this manual are examples from a test copy of the system.

Each screenshot has yellow numbered markers. The list next to the picture explains each number, in the order you would use them. Part 1

Parents and guardians

Thuli enrols Amahle

Everything a parent or guardian does: applying, paying, keeping your child's details up to date and following their attendance, reports and fees.

1.1 The website

The Fees page lists every package and what it costs; the Timetable page shows the centre's opening hours and classes. You don't need an account to look at these.

1.2 Create your account and sign in

1.3 Apply for your child

From your portal, tap Start enrolment (or Enrol another child under My learners). The application has six steps. Nothing is charged now: you only pay once the centre accepts the application.

Tip No school report yet (for example, a Grade R or Grade 1 learner)? Contact the centre before you apply.

1.4 The aptitude test

Straight after you send the application, your child does a short activity of reading questions and puzzles. It helps the tutor know where to start; it is not a pass or fail test. Hand the phone or computer to your child for this part (see Part 2 for what they see).

  • Younger children may need you to read the questions aloud. Please don't help with the answers.
  • Your child can stop and come back later; the answers so far are saved.
  • If you don't do the test now, your portal shows Go to the test until it is done.

1.5 Waiting for the centre

The centre reads the report card and the test result, then does one of these:

What happensWhat you see
AcceptedA notification, and the Pay with PayShap button on your Overview. You have 7 days to pay, or the offer lapses.
More information neededA message explaining what is needed, for example a clearer report card. Use Replace report card on your Overview.
Test againThe centre can let your child take the test again.
DeclinedA message with the reason.

1.6 Pay with PayShap

Important Skolopals never asks for your card details, and never asks you to pay into a different account. If anyone does, contact the centre.
Tip If the centre can't match your payment (for example the amount doesn't match), you get a message with the reason and the button changes to Upload new proof.

1.7 Timetable and online classes

  • The link opens 15 minutes before the class starts and closes when it ends.
  • Joining tells the tutor your child is there. The tutor still confirms the register at the end of class, so make sure your child stays for the whole lesson.
  • If you have more than one child in the class, you are asked who is joining.
  • Hybrid classes (some at the centre, some online) work the same way for those joining online.

1.8 My learners: details and safety

On your child's page you can update their details, their emergency contacts, who may collect them and whether they may leave on their own.

Important If someone who is not on the list arrives to collect your child, staff will not release your child. They will phone you first. Keep the list and your phone number up to date.

1.9 Attendance and notifications

If your child is still signed in 30 minutes after their class ends, you and the centre get a "still at the centre" notification.

1.10 Reports, fees, messages and resources

Monthly invoices are issued on the 15th for the month ahead and are due on the 1st. Pay each one with PayShap in the same way as the first invoice.

Part 2

Learners

Amahle takes the test; Neo uses his portal

What a learner sees: the aptitude test when they apply, and their own portal if the centre gives them a login.

2.1 The aptitude test

Your grown-up starts the test for you after sending your application. There are 25 questions about reading and puzzles, and it takes about 20 minutes.

  • It's not a pass or fail test. Some questions get harder as you go, and that's fine.
  • You can stop and carry on later. Your answers are saved.
  • When you finish, hand the device back to your grown-up.

2.2 Your own login

Older learners can get their own login. Ask the centre: an administrator sets it up and you receive an email to choose a password. Your portal shows only your own classes, reports and messages. It never shows fees, or anything about brothers or sisters.

Part 3

Tutors

Johan runs the door and keeps the register

Your classes, the door at the start and end of class, the attendance register, assessments and reports.

3.1 Getting started

The centre adds you as a tutor. You receive an email with a link to set your password. Sign in at the same address as parents; you land in the Tutor workspace.

3.2 At the door (classes at the centre)

Use your phone at the entrance. Open Attendance register and choose today's session; the At the door card lists every learner placed in the class.

For staff Left on their own only appears if a parent has allowed this child to leave alone.
Important Anyone still signed in 30 minutes after their class ends triggers a "still at the centre" alert to the administrators and the child's parents. Always sign learners out.

3.3 The attendance register

Parents see the register the same day.

3.4 Online and hybrid classes

Parents and learners join through a Skolopals Join link, which records who opened it. At the end of the class, check the list against who was really in the call and confirm the register.

Centre administrators

Lindiwe runs the centre

Applications, payments, classes, the door, billing and the reports behind the centre. Every menu item is covered in order.

4.1 Dashboard

4.2 Users

For staff Parents normally sign up themselves on the website. Add a tutor here first, then give them their subjects under Tutors.

4.3 Applications

Under the test result, Let them take the test again gives the learner a fresh attempt.

4.4 Payments

  1. Open the proof of payment and check the money is really in the centre's account.
  2. Check the amount and the date, and add the bank reference.
  3. Tap Verify payment, then Yes, verify. For a first invoice the learner is enrolled and placed in classes straight away, and the family is told.
  4. If you can't find the payment, choose a reason under Reject. The family sees the reason and can upload new proof.

4.5 Learners

4.6 Tutors

4.7 Timetable: classes and placing learners

For staff When a payment is verified, the learner is placed automatically where a suitable class has room. Anyone left over appears under Applications → Needs placement; place them here.

4.8 Sessions: cancellations and cover

  • Cancel session with a reason (for example load-shedding). Tell the families is ticked by default. A cancelled session can be put back on.
  • Save cover assigns a substitute tutor. Only tutors recorded for that subject at every grade in the class's language are offered. The substitute can see the class list from a week before.

4.9 At the door

Administrators use the same sign-in and sign-out controls as tutors (see 3.2). The Still at the centre box lists anyone signed in but not out. To let a new person collect a child after a parent agrees on the phone, add them to the child's collectors from the learner's page.

4.10 Billing

  • Run the monthly invoices on the 15th, for the month ahead. Running it twice is safe; nobody is billed twice.
  • Apply late fees adds the R150 fee to invoices more than three days past due.
  • PayShap details are what parents see when they pay. Check them carefully.

Frequently asked questions

I didn't get the confirmation email.
Check your spam or promotions folder. The link expires; sign in and ask for a new one, or contact the centre.
I forgot my password.
Tap Forgot password? on the sign-in page (1.2).
My child didn't finish the test.
Your Overview shows Go to the test. Your child carries on from where they stopped.
I paid but forgot the reference.
Upload the proof anyway and put the date and amount. The centre will match it.
My proof of payment was rejected.
The reason is shown on your Overview. Check it, then tap Upload new proof.
What happens if I don't pay within 7 days?
The offer lapses. Contact the centre; they can accept the application again with a fresh invoice.
Someone else needs to collect my child today.
Add them under My learners → Edit details → Authorised to collect with the last 4 digits of their ID, before they arrive. Otherwise staff will phone you and won't release your child until the person is added.
The Join link says it isn't open.
It opens 15 minutes before the class. Check the time on your timetable.
My child was marked absent but was there.
Send a message to the tutor or the centre from Messages.
How do I enrol a second child?
My learners → Enrol another child.
Can my child have their own login?
Yes, ask the centre (2.2).

Your information (POPIA)

Skolopals Tutoring Centre is the responsible party for the personal information in the system, under the Protection of Personal Information Act 4 of 2013. The full privacy notice is on the website (Privacy, at the bottom of every page).

  • Consent first. We don't register a learner, run the test or place a child in a class without a parent's consent. Optional consents (photos in marketing, WhatsApp and email updates) never affect a child's place, and you can withdraw any consent.
  • Who sees what. You see only your own children. Tutors see their learners' names, grades, academic records and attendance, never your fees. The centre's administrators see everything needed to run the centre. Other parents see nothing about your family. The database enforces these limits.
  • No health information. We don't collect information about health, allergies or medical conditions. If your child has a need we should know about to keep them safe, tell the centre manager in person.
  • Stored outside South Africa. Our systems are hosted in London, United Kingdom, under protection substantially similar to POPIA.
  • How long we keep it. Test results, invoices, payments, messages and learner records for 5 years; enquiries that didn't lead to enrolment for 1 year. Then we delete or anonymise them.
  • Your rights. Ask what we hold and get a copy; correct or delete anything wrong; object to processing; opt out of marketing. We respond within 30 days, free of charge.
