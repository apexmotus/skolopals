Welcome

This guide shows how someone becomes a Skolopals tutor and teaches their first online lesson: from the day the centre opens applications to the moment the tutor confirms who attended.

It follows one person. Palesa Mokoena sees that the centre is looking for Mathematics tutors, applies on her phone, is approved by the centre administrator Lindiwe, and teaches an online Maths class. Amahle Mthembu (Grade 6) joins the class, with her mother Thuli. All names, numbers and documents in this guide are examples from a test copy of the system.

Common questions, and how Skolopals looks after applicants' information.

Frequently asked questions

I can't see Apply to teach on the website.
The centre isn't recruiting at the moment. Check again later, or get in touch through the Contact page.
I already have a Skolopals account as a parent. Can I apply?
Yes. Choose I already have one and sign in. If you're approved, your account keeps your children.
I never got the confirmation email.
Check your spam or promotions folder. If it still hasn't arrived, or the link has expired, contact the centre and they will help you confirm your account.
Can I change my application after sending it?
No. Contact the centre with the change. Withdrawing doesn't help: you can apply only once each time the centre opens applications.
My upload says it didn't finish.
Check your connection and try again. Files must be a PDF, JPG, PNG or iPhone photo, up to 10 MB. The selfie and the back of an ID card must be photos.
I was approved but I still see the application page.
Sign out and sign in again. Your account changes to a tutor account when you next sign in.
A learner joined but isn't marked present.
The mark is only a suggestion until you confirm it. Change it on the register before you tap Confirm register.

Your information (POPIA)

  • Skolopals uses an applicant's details, ID, selfie and documents only to assess the application and confirm who they are.
  • Only the centre's administrators can see them. The ID and selfie are never shown publicly.
  • References are contacted only with the applicant's agreement, given when they apply.
  • If an application is not successful or is withdrawn, it is deleted, with every document, 12 months after the decision. An administrator confirms each deletion, and it is logged.
  • If the applicant becomes a tutor, the documents are kept with their staff record.
